# Ikiomoye Emmanuel

### Computing educator and researcher

I am a Senior Lecturer in Computing at University of Bolton Manchester and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of South Africa. My work connects applied AI, intelligent networking and practical computing education.

## Research interests

- **Applied AI and predictive analytics:** building and evaluating models for practical decision-making.
- **Responsible and human-centred AI:** explainability, transparent evaluation and meaningful use of model outputs.
- **Intelligent networking:** SD-WAN, traffic analysis and adaptive path selection.
- **Interdisciplinary applications:** healthcare, energy, education and researcher discovery.

## Featured project

### [SD-WAN Controller](https://github.com/eikiomoye/SD-WAN-Controller)

A Python project exploring flow-level path selection, bandwidth admission and recovery from link failures using synthetic telemetry. It includes a deterministic simulation, baseline policies, automated tests and reproducible examples.

The simulator is a new portfolio implementation. Its results are illustrative and are separate from my doctoral experiments and published findings.

## Teaching and academic practice

My teaching spans programming, software development, databases, cybersecurity, cloud networking and AI. I am interested in practical laboratory learning, clear assessment design and helping students connect computing principles with working systems.
