# Ikiomoye Emmanuel

### Computing educator and researcher

I am a Lecturer in Computing at University of Greater Manchester and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of South Africa. My work connects applied AI, intelligent networking and practical computing education.

## Research interests

- **Applied AI and predictive analytics:** building and evaluating models for practical decision-making.
- **Responsible and human-centred AI:** explainability, transparent evaluation and meaningful use of model outputs.
- **Intelligent networking:** SD-WAN, traffic analysis and adaptive path selection.
- **Interdisciplinary applications:** healthcare, energy, education and researcher discovery.

## Featured projects

### [SD-WAN: Doctoral Research and Python Simulator](https://github.com/eikiomoye/SD-WAN-Controller)

My doctoral research explored an enhanced SD-WAN architecture for dynamic load balancing, using traffic monitors and controller-driven route updates. The [PhD research guide](https://github.com/eikiomoye/SD-WAN-Controller/tree/main/research/thesis) documents the architecture, experimental settings and selected reported findings with thesis page references.

The repository also includes a new Python simulator for flow-level path selection, bandwidth admission and recovery from link failures, with baseline policies, automated tests and reproducible synthetic examples. The original doctoral scripts are no longer available; the simulator is a separate implementation, and its results do not reproduce the PhD experiments.

### [ResearchMatch](https://github.com/eikiomoye/researchmatch)

An NLP-based researcher-discovery application that uses semantic embeddings and MongoDB Atlas Vector Search to identify researchers with related expertise. It supports keyword and document-based searches and presents relevant publications to help explain matches.

My fork: [`eikiomoye/researchmatch`](https://github.com/eikiomoye/researchmatch). Original repository: [`danielsegb/researchmatch`](https://github.com/danielsegb/researchmatch).

### [XAI Healthcare Survey Companion](https://github.com/eikiomoye/XAI-Healthcare-Review)

A research companion to our 2024 survey on explainable AI in healthcare, with 131 extracted references, method and evaluation summaries, a reading guide and a Python catalogue-search utility. These materials were created after publication and are distinguished from the original review's screening data.

### [AI for Sustainable Development in Africa](https://github.com/eikiomoye/AI-Sustainable-Development-Africa)

A companion to our 2026 structured review, organising the adaptive deployment framework, 72 bibliography entries, resource dimensions and reported study-selection counts. It includes a new, unscored discussion worksheet and Python utilities. The framework is conceptual and has not been empirically validated.

## Selected publications

**[Comparative Classification of DNS-over-HTTPS Traffic on the CIRACIC-DoHBrw-2020 Dataset Using Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost](https://doi.org/10.63158/journalisi.v8i5.1914)**  
Ikiomoye Douglas Emmanuel. *Journal of Information Systems and Informatics*, 8(5), 6503-6534 (2026).

A controlled comparison of three classifier families for identifying DoH within encrypted HTTPS traffic, examining predictive performance, computational cost and permutation-based feature dependence. The findings concern within-benchmark classification; they do not establish operational or cross-network generalisation.

**[Research code and results](https://github.com/eikiomoye/DoH-Traffic-Classification)**: reviewed notebook and Python runner, supplied historical results, citation files and automated consistency checks. The full benchmark has not been rerun during the code review.
